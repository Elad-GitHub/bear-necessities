Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

The short version. We collect what we need to run a members-only community map: your account details, what you post, and how the app is used. We don't sell your data. Some of it is processed by services we rely on (hosting, maps, photo storage and, for the trip planner, an AI service). You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time.

1. Who is responsible

Bear Necessities ("we", "us") is responsible for the personal data described here. Contact us at privacy@bearnecessities.app.

2. What we collect

  • Account details: name, email address, password (stored only as a secure hash), the social profile link you give us for membership review, and your membership status.
  • Profile and content: profile photo and bio, places and events you submit or suggest edits to, reviews, photos, messages to other members, RSVPs and saved items.
  • Trip planning: the area, interests and trip description you enter in the trip planner.
  • Usage information: pages viewed, buttons used, errors and similar events, linked to a random session id and, when you are signed in, your account; plus technical data such as IP address and browser type in server logs.
  • Stored on your device: sign-in tokens and a few preferences (such as your last map view) in your browser's local storage. We don't use advertising cookies.

We don't ask for your precise device location unless you choose to share it in a feature that needs it.

3. Why we use it

  • To run the Service: your account, the map, listings, messages, reviews and trip plans.
  • To review membership applications and keep the community safe, including moderating content and preventing abuse.
  • To understand how the Service is used and fix problems.
  • To contact you about your account, and about important changes.
  • To meet legal obligations and defend legal claims.

We rely on performing our agreement with you (the Terms of Use), our legitimate interest in running and protecting a safe community, your consent where we ask for it, and legal obligations.

4. Who we share it with

We share personal data only as needed with:

  • Other members: what you choose to make public, such as your display name, profile photo, reviews, photos and listings.
  • Service providers that run parts of the Service for us, such as hosting and databases, Cloudflare (photo storage), Mapbox (maps and address search), and Google (the Gemini AI service used by the trip planner).
  • Authorities when the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety.

We don't sell personal data and don't share it for advertising.

5. The AI trip planner

When the AI planner is available, your trip description, the interests you picked, the area, and the list of candidate places are sent to Google's Gemini service to produce your plan. We don't send your name or email. We use Google's free service tier, under which Google may use this content to improve its products. Please don't write personal or sensitive information in your trip description. If the AI service is busy or unavailable, the plan is made by our own built-in planner instead and nothing is sent to Google.

6. International transfers

Our providers may process data in other countries, including the United States and the European Union. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

7. How long we keep it

  • Account data: for as long as your account exists, and then deleted or anonymised within a reasonable period, except what we must keep by law or for an open dispute.
  • Content you post: until you or we delete it, or your account is deleted.
  • Usage and log data: for a limited period needed for security and analysis.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under Israel's Privacy Protection Law and, where it applies, the EU and UK GDPR), you can ask to see the personal data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, get a copy of it, and withdraw consent you gave. Write to privacy@bearnecessities.app; we may need to confirm it's you. You can also complain to your local data-protection authority.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, such as encrypted connections, hashed passwords and restricted access. No service is completely secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

10. Children

The Service is for adults aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone younger.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. If a change is significant we will tell members in the Service or by email before it takes effect.
